# Branding intake checklist

Collect (or propose-and-confirm from her Human Design + business context) before
generating. Don't block on perfection — for anything missing, suggest a default
grounded in her chart/business and get a yes.

## Required-ish
- [ ] **Brand / business name**
- [ ] **One line:** what she does + for whom
- [ ] **Primary goal of this page** (book a call? sell a product? waitlist? email?)
- [ ] **The single primary CTA** (one — don't dilute)
- [ ] **Audience**: who, their pain, their aspiration
- [ ] **Tone words** (3–5 adjectives: e.g. cálido, sereno, premium, terrenal, sagrado)
- [ ] **Copy language & voice** (e.g. español rioplatense, cercano, sin jerga)

## Visual direction
- [ ] **Palette** — hexes, or a named direction ("warm earth", "moon & bone",
      "sunset clay"). Default for this niche: cream/sand/oak + one warm accent.
- [ ] **Typography** — named fonts or "elegí vos". Premium-editorial defaults:
      Playfair Display / Libre Caslon / EB Garamond (headlines) + DM Sans / Inter
      (body). (Full enum in [stitch-api.md](stitch-api.md).)
- [ ] **Imagery** — photography style / illustration / none. Describe subject &
      light if photographic ("warm natural light, no people, sculptural objects").
- [ ] **Shape language** — sharp / soft / pill (maps to Stitch `roundness`).
- [ ] **Anti-references** — what she does NOT want to look like
      ("nada de SaaS azul, nada corporativo, sin stock genérico").

## Structure
- [ ] **Sections, in order** (hero → … → closing CTA)
- [ ] **Device** — desktop, mobile, or both
- [ ] **Reference material** — screenshots she loves / links to admire
      (you'll translate these into prompt language, not clone them)

## Assets she may already have
- [ ] Logo (Stitch can emit an SVG logo screen, or she uploads one later)
- [ ] Existing copy / testimonials / offer details / pricing
- [ ] Domain / where it will live (informs the "what next" conversation)
